# Homepage Design Plan

## Goal

Make the first page feel like a premium commercial storefront, not a generic bakery template.

## Ready-To-Go Sections

1. Hero
   - Clear luxury positioning.
   - Primary call to action: `View collection`.
   - Secondary call to action: `Compare logo routes`.

2. Trust Signals
   - Small-batch baking.
   - Gift-ready packaging.
   - Ingredient transparency.
   - Secure checkout roadmap.

3. Product Collection
   - Brown Butter Chocolate.
   - Matcha White Chocolate.
   - Strawberry Shortbread.

4. Brand Direction
   - Atelier Wordmark.
   - Embossed Seal.
   - Minimal Bite.
   - Ribbon Monogram.

5. Commercial UI Guide
   - High-end design steps shown directly on the page for now.

6. Checkout Preview
   - Launch offer: free delivery over `$45`.
   - Promo code: `SWEETSTART`.

## Later Cleanup Candidate

When the site becomes customer-facing, move internal logo and UI planning sections out of the homepage and keep them only in `docs/design`.
