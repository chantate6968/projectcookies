# Browse Function

## Customer Goal

Customers can discover the cookie collection, understand the brand, and choose a product to inspect or buy.

## Scope

- Homepage collection preview.
- Product listing page.
- Collection filters such as flavor, gift type, and availability.
- Product cards with price, quantity, short description, and primary action.

## First Build Slice

Create a browseable product catalog using structured cookie data.

## Design Requirements

- Keep product cards clean and editorial.
- Avoid cluttered sale badges.
- Show high-end signals: ingredient story, packaging, and freshness.

## Verification

- Product cards are readable on mobile.
- Every product has a name, price, quantity, description, and image/visual placeholder.
- Browse actions lead toward product details or cart.
