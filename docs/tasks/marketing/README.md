# Marketing Function

## Customer Goal

Customers understand why `lamsumsum cookies` is worth buying, gifting, and trusting.

## Scope

- Homepage hero.
- Launch promotion.
- Seasonal campaigns.
- Reviews and testimonials.
- FAQ.
- Corporate gifting and special occasions.

## First Build Slice

Polish the homepage marketing story and add a reviews/FAQ section.

## Design Requirements

- Keep copy refined and specific.
- Use one primary promotion at a time.
- Avoid generic claims like "best cookies ever" unless supported by reviews.

## Verification

- The homepage explains product, audience, and offer quickly.
- The primary call to action is clear.
- Trust signals are visible before checkout.
